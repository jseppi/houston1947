Houston 1947

Houston's 1947 Zoning Plan, Then and Now

Houston never adopted zoning. Comparing the city's 1947 plan to 2026 land use, more than half of the land it zoned for homes and for industry is used that way today, and business districts match three times as often as chance would predict.

Scroll to explore ↓

The plan

On 15 December 1947, the City of Houston published a Zoning District Map laying out ten cumulative districts, A through J — from First Dwelling to Heavy Industrial. Each district permitted every use allowed in the districts before it, plus more.

Houston never adopted this plan. Voters have rejected zoning multiple times since, and the city remains the largest in the United States without a conventional zoning ordinance.

Decoding the map

The districts are marked only by printed screen patterns — stipples, hatches, crosshatches — not color. We classified every part of the scan automatically, matching each patch's texture to one of the ten legend patterns with a random-forest classifier.

Checked against an independent, blind visual reading of 270 sample points, the automatic classification is correct for … of the mapped area at the Residential / Commercial / Industrial group level.

How close is close?

If we deliberately shift the georeferenced 1947 layer sideways, agreement should fall — and it does, monotonically, which is independent evidence the registration is sound.

Method & caveats

  • The 1947 districts were digitized automatically from a scanned paper map by texture classification, then checked against a blind visual reading of 270 stratified sample points (made by a single rater, an AI vision model directed by the analyst): about 86% correct at the group level — high for residential and industrial, but only about 42% of the area mapped as commercial is truly commercial on the 1947 map, so treat the commercial figures with more caution.
  • The scan was georeferenced with 32 ground-control points; the registration's check-point error is about 54 ft, with roughly 30% of the study area lying outside the control-point hull and therefore less certain.
  • Undeveloped, unknown, and vacant parcels and rights-of-way are excluded from every percentage's denominator.
  • Every reported group difference beats 400 random spatial shifts of the 1947 layer.
